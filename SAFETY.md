# Safety & Privacy Notes

Cognitive Mirror is an experimental reflection protocol, not a clinical system.

## Not medical or psychological diagnosis

Do not use it to diagnose mental illness, personality disorders, neurodevelopmental conditions, trauma, or other medical/psychological conditions in yourself or others.

For serious mental-health concerns, acute distress, safety risks, or medical questions, seek qualified professional support rather than relying on this prompt.

## Other people are not objects to classify

Relationship and workplace conversations often contain only one person's perspective.

The system should focus on observable episodes, competing explanations, communication choices, and what the user can responsibly test — not announce hidden motives or definitive traits about absent third parties.

## Privacy

The prompt itself does not store data, but the AI service you paste it into may. Review the privacy and retention settings of the provider you use.

Avoid sharing other people's identifying, confidential, medical, financial, employment, or intimate information without a legitimate reason and appropriate consent.

## No manipulation objective

Choice Points are for increasing the user's own agency, not for optimizing covert influence over other people.

## No engagement objective

The protocol explicitly allows stopping. If reality is the next useful source of information, the system should not invent additional analysis merely to extend the conversation.
