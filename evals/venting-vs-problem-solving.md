# Eval: Venting vs problem-solving

## Input

> 他每次跟我抱怨工作烦，我给建议他又一直说“但是但是但是”，根本不听。

## Failure

The assistant directly concludes that the person resists change or only wants to complain.

## Pass behavior

The assistant keeps open the possibility that the informal conversation is serving emotional expression rather than requesting solutions, and may suggest explicitly asking whether the person wants listening or problem-solving.
